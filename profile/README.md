# The Fermart Lab
### Biointegrated Materials and Engineering

**Where biology meets materials science.**

Welcome to the GitHub organization of the **Biointegrated Materials and Engineering Lab (Fermart Lab)** at the **Institute for Bioengineering of Catalonia (IBEC)**, Barcelona, Spain.

Our research explores how biological principles, natural materials, and engineering can be integrated to develop new technologies, with a particular focus on sustainability, advanced manufacturing, and the next generation of biointegrated materials.

We envision a transition toward a **Biomaterial Age**, in which biological concepts and components become fundamental elements of technological design and manufacturing.

🌐 **[Lab Website](https://jgfermart.com/)**

---

## Research Areas

Our interdisciplinary research combines materials science, biology, computational methods, and engineering.

**Bioinspired and Biointegrated Materials**

Understanding natural materials and biological structures to develop new materials and engineering approaches.

**Sustainable Materials and Circular Manufacturing**

Developing biologically derived materials and manufacturing strategies that incorporate degradation, recycling, and ecological compatibility into their design.

**Computational Modeling and Machine Learning**

Applying numerical methods, computational tools, and artificial intelligence to the analysis, prediction, and design of biological and material systems.

**Advanced Manufacturing and Biofabrication**

Integrating biological processes, digital fabrication, and engineering techniques to create functional materials and structures.
